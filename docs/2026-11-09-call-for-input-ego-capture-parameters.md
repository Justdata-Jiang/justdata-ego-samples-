# Collect Once, Cover What Everyone Needs: An Open Call for Input on Ego Data Capture Parameters

In embodied intelligence data collection, there is a quiet but costly waste: for the same kind of scenario, different teams buy their own equipment, set their own parameters, and go to the same field to collect it all over again. Factories, hotels, home care, agriculture, logistics — nearly every scenario has been walked through repeatedly by separate groups. You collect yours, I collect mine: field of view differs, frame rate differs, calibration differs, and in the end the datasets cannot be stitched together, so downstream users clean and adapt each one on their own. Human effort and budget are spent on redundant collection.

We want to try to change this. If we are all collecting anyway, why not state our needs first and align on a plan before anyone spends another day in the field? So JUST DATA is making a public call: **collect once, and cover as much of what everyone needs as possible**. This is the work we are doing ourselves — building multi-view ego data into one unified, adjustable collection paradigm, so that a single collection run serves many downstream needs instead of forcing every consumer to start from scratch.

To that end, we are openly soliciting your requirements for capture parameters, including but not limited to:

- **FOV (field of view)**: What horizontal and vertical field of view do you need? Monocular or multi-view, and how should the views be arranged?
- **Frame rate and resolution**: What frame rate and resolution do training and simulation each require?
- **IMU specifications**: Do you need inertial data, and at what accuracy and sampling rate?
- **Modalities**: Do you need synchronized depth, audio, eye tracking, or other modalities?
- **Intrinsic/extrinsic calibration**: To what precision, in what delivery format, and do you need on-site calibration procedures?

The way to contribute is open: raise your needs directly in our public discussion channels (issues, forum, or voting), and feel free to add dimensions we have not listed. We are not presupposing any answer. We want to gather what people actually need, then reason together, converge, and distill a unified, adjustable collection plan. Convergence matters more than volume here: a plan that reflects ten genuinely different requirements is more valuable than a list with a hundred votes but no alignment.

We also want to offer something back to those who engage deeply. Contributors who put in substantial, meaningful input may be invited to take part in defining our collection standard, and may in the future access the data we collect at better pricing and with faster delivery.

One honest boundary: we will not fabricate specific model numbers, costs, or undisclosed technical details in order to look more polished. What we want is a real, workable industry consensus, not a tidy list nobody uses. Collecting once and serving many depends on openness and honesty, not slogans — so we will publish what we learn and make the convergence process as transparent as we can.

If this resonates — whether you are a robotics lab, a foundation-model team, or an individual researcher — we would like to hear from you. Tell us what you need from ego data. We will read every requirement, and we will turn what we learn into a capture plan that is shared, not owned.
