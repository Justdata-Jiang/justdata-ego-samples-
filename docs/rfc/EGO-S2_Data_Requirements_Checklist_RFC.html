Ground rules
  • No pricing in this document. Unit prices, volumes and payment terms are out of scope.
  • Options marked baseline are starting points for discussion, not requirements. Options marked caveat carry a known technical risk.
  • Items marked Hard gate are the ones believed impossible to trade away without changing what the data can be used for. Being proven wrong here is especially welcome.
  • Evidence is preferred: a paper, a datasheet, a measurement, or a deployment story.

How to contribute

Copy the checklist into a new Issue and tick your answers with - [x], or fork and open a PR to change an item, a threshold or the wording. One item per Issue is easiest to discuss — cite the item ID (e.g. C1). You can also use Copy summary above to paste a full configuration.

How to read the result

Nothing here is a requirement yet. The purpose of this RFC is to find out which of these 54 questions the community thinks belong in a real data contract, and which are over-engineering. The most useful outcome would be a shorter list with better justified thresholds.
